# Personal Website
It's a constant work and progress; I like to take what I'm learning in front-end development and find a creative way to incorporate it into my website. 
It may be safe to say that minimalism ist nicht so mein Ding. 

Writing and video live at `/journal/` on `fmird.com`. Article drafts, account configuration, and the static content builder are maintained in the separate Website coordination workspace. Only rendered public pages are committed here; GitHub Pages serves them directly from `master` without a runtime build. Articles can later link to a full YouTube video and short clips.
